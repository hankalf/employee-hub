Tiles

The buttons on the kiosk. Paste a monday.com form share link into each one; forms open inside the hub and the kiosk returns home afterwards. Tap counts show how often each tile was used.

Drag ⠿ to reorder (or focus it and use the arrow keys). To move a tile to another group, edit the tile.

    Settings

    Kiosks, your logo, how the kiosk looks, the hub's name and languages, and backups. Each section saves on its own.

    Kiosks

    Every tablet, screen, scanner or phone showing the kiosk checks in once a minute. Name your kiosks so you can tell them apart, choose which tiles each one shows and its layout, and tick Alert to be told when one goes quiet. Phones that only scanned the QR code drop off the list after 30 days.

    KioskStatusShowsLayoutAlert

    Alerts

    Get told when a kiosk with Alert ticked stops checking in, and when the daily link check finds a form that stopped working. Send alerts as a new item on a monday.com board, to a webhook (Slack, Microsoft Teams, or Zapier/Make to turn it into an email), or both.

    Admins

    You're the owner: the admin login whose password is ADMIN_PASSWORD on Railway. Give everyone else their own login, so the activity log shows who changed what. Admins can change everything except other admins and restoring backups.

    Backup and restore

    A backup is a zip with every tile, group, note and setting, the logo and tile images, tap counts, the kiosk list and admin logins (passwords are stored scrambled). One is made automatically every night and the last 7 are kept on the server. Restoring replaces everything; the content from just before is kept on the server too.
