Tiles

The buttons on the kiosk. Paste a monday.com form share link into each one; forms open inside the hub and the kiosk returns home afterwards. Use the arrows to change the order.

    Settings

    The note on the kiosk, your logo, how the kiosk looks, and the hub's name and languages. Each section saves on its own.

    Note on the kiosk

    Shown beside the greeting at the top of the kiosk page. Use it for announcements, reminders or safety messages.

    Logo

    Shown in the top bar of the kiosk, on the QR page and here. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best; it is shown about 44 px tall. Max 2 MB. If the logo has dark text, set the theme below to Always light, or it will vanish on the dark theme.
