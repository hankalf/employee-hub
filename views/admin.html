Tiles

The buttons on the kiosk. Paste a monday.com form share link into each one; forms open inside the hub and the kiosk returns home afterwards. Tap counts show how often each tile was used.

Notes

Announcements shown beside the greeting on the kiosk and on the screensaver. Give a note dates to schedule it ahead of time; several can show at once, in this order.

    Settings

    Kiosks, your logo, how the kiosk looks, the hub's name and languages, and backups. Each section saves on its own.

    Kiosks

    Every tablet or screen showing the kiosk checks in once a minute. Give each one a name so you can tell them apart; if one goes quiet, check its power and Wi-Fi.

    Logo

    Shown in the top bar of the kiosk, on the screensaver, on the QR page and here. A PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best; it is shown about 44 px tall. Max 2 MB. If the logo has dark text, set the theme below to Always light, or it will vanish on the dark theme.

    Backup and restore

    The backup is a zip with every tile, group, note, setting, the logo, tap counts and the kiosk list. Keep a copy somewhere safe, for example before big changes. Restoring replaces everything with the zip's contents; the settings from just before are kept on the server.

    Schedule

    Uses the kiosk's own clock. An end time earlier than the start runs overnight (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00).

    Leads only

    The tile shows a lock and a keypad. The link stays hidden from anyone without the PIN; after the right PIN it opens for 15 minutes on that device.

    Add group

    Groups put a heading over a set of tiles, e.g. Safety, Time off, Equipment. Tiles without a group are shown first.

    Add note

    Leave the dates empty to show the note until you switch it off.
